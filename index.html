Our story

Inspired by nature, purified by technology.

HYDRELON launched in 2026 as the consumer brand of Hercules Investments LLC, a New Jersey company. One idea sits behind every product: clean water and clean air are the foundation of a healthy home, and the technology to deliver them should be simple, well-made and within reach.

We design across six categories — water filtration, air purification, vitamin shower heads, eyeglass lens care and refrigerator filters — and ship every order from our own warehouse in the United States.
